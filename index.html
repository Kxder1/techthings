Earbuds and headphones are some of the best-selling tech on Amazon almost every week. The lists above show which ones are selling and on sale right now, from budget picks to noise cancelling headphones.

Best-selling gadgets make easy gifts because lots of people already own and like them. Earbuds, headphones, game controllers and gaming headsets are popular tech gifts for teens, adults and gamers.

Where do the products come from?
